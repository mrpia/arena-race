# Game ideas

*Deutsche Version: [IDEEN.md](IDEEN.md)*

Stuck? Pick a theme, then pick one twist. You can always change the theme later:
swapping cats for dragons takes seconds. New rules take longer, so add them one at a time.

Right now the game has two players who can walk around and hop (Space / Enter).
Everything below starts from there.

## Battle games

**🥷 Ninja food fight**
Two chefs throw tomatoes, pies and spaghetti at each other. Three hits and you're covered in sauce.
- a banana peel that makes you slide
- a giant cake power-up
- splat sounds

**🐉 Dragon vs. dragon**
Two dragons breathe fireballs across a castle courtyard. Walls crumble when they get hit.
- an ice dragon and a fire dragon with different powers
- eggs that hatch little helpers

**🧙 Wizard duel**
Each wizard has three spells on three keys: fireball, shield, freeze.
- invent the spell names ("Sneezius Maximus")
- a magic bar that runs out and fills up again
- a spell that turns the other player into a frog for 3 seconds

**🤖 Robot sumo**
No shooting. Push the other robot out of the ring!
- slippery ice patches
- a magnet power-up
- the ring gets smaller and smaller

## Race and collect games

**🐱 Cat chaos**
Two cats race around a living room knocking things off shelves. Whoever breaks the most in 60 seconds wins.
- a vacuum cleaner that chases both cats
- extra points for the expensive vase

**🍕 Pizza delivery rush**
Grab pizzas from the shop and deliver them to the house that lights up, before they get cold.
- cars driving across the street
- steal the other player's pizza

**🦖 Dino egg hunt**
Collect eggs that pop up in the jungle. A T-rex chases whoever is winning.
- golden eggs worth 5 points
- hide in the bushes

**👻 Ghost tag**
One player is a ghost, the other a ghost hunter. Swap roles when you get caught.
- the ghost can turn invisible for a moment
- the hunter has a flashlight

## Team games

Both players are on the same team and play against the game instead of each other.
You need something to beat together: a timer, spreading lava, or monsters that the
computer moves. A timer is the quickest to build. Monsters take a few more steps.

**🎂 Save the birthday cake**
Ants march in from the edges toward a cake in the middle. Bump them away before they eat it. If the cake is gone, you both lose.
- more and faster ants every round
- a fly swatter power-up
- the cake gets smaller with every bite

**🌋 Lava escape**
On the lava floor, the glowing pools slowly grow. Both of you have to stay on the rock for 60 seconds.
- if one player falls in, the other can pull them out
- rocks that sink if you stand on them too long
- the lava bubbles faster near the end

**🗺️ Treasure dig**
On the treasure map, both players stand on the X and press their action keys to dig. The chest is too heavy for one, so you carry it to the ship together.
- pirates who try to grab the chest
- a second X that's a trap
- a parrot that shouts where to dig

**🌕 Moon rescue**
Your rocket broke down on the moon. Find the five missing parts before your air runs out.
- meteors that show a shadow where they're about to land
- each player can carry only one part at a time
- craters to hide in

**🐧 Penguin round-up**
Baby penguins slide around the frozen lake. Push them into the igloo together.
- cracks in the ice that keep growing
- a seal that steals penguins
- a golden penguin worth 5 points

**🤝 Any game as a team game**
Turn a battle or race game into a team game with one of these:
- one score for both, or one row of hearts you share
- tie the players together with a rope
- a door that only opens while both players stand on a button
- if a player gets knocked out, the other brings them back by touching them

## Strange but great

**🧀 Mouse vs. cat**
The mouse is small and fast and grabs cheese. The cat is big and slow and tries to catch it.
Good when one player is much younger or older than the other.

**⚽ Slime soccer**
Two blobs push a ball into goals.
- a bouncier ball
- two balls at once
- goals that move

## Small things to add to any game

Start with these if you want to see something change right away:

- change the emojis and colors of the players
- make the players faster, slower or bigger
- change the color of the floor
- add a score at the top
- add coins or stars to collect
- make the action key shoot instead of hop
- add walls or rocks in the arena
- add a 60-second timer
- show a "Player 1 wins!" screen with a "Play again" button
- add sounds
- write your names on the title screen

## How to ask

Say exactly what you want to see, one thing at a time. For example:

> Change Player 1 into a dragon 🐉 and Player 2 into a unicorn 🦄.

> When a player presses the action key, shoot a fireball 🔥 in the direction they are walking. If it hits the other player, give the shooter one point.

> Add 5 stars ⭐ in random places. When a player touches a star it disappears, the player gets a point, and a new star appears somewhere else.

> The game is too easy. Make the fireballs a bit slower and let each player shoot only once per second.

If something goes wrong, describe what you see:

> The dragon can walk through the rocks. It should stop at the rocks.

Then play it, laugh, and decide what's next.
